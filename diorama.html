虹桥On the Rainbow Bridge

The bridge, its crowd, the big boat and the near bank are cut out of the painting and stood up at their own depths, so they shift against each other as you move. The crowded street on the left bank stands up in strips, like the flats of a paper theatre. Nothing is repainted: where a cut-out used to cover the silk, the gap is filled with blank silk.

Your walker is a porter with two baskets on a shoulder pole, borrowed from the street outside Dr. Zhao’s shop near the end of the scroll. He is mirrored when he walks to the right.

Look around only a little: the cut-outs are flat, and from too far to the side they show it.
