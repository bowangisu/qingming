虹桥Walk the Rainbow Bridge

The river and its banks are the painting itself, laid back down on the ground it shows: from the painter’s own direction they line up with the scroll exactly. The bridge is rebuilt as a simple arch and dressed in the painted bridge’s own railing and timbers, so you can walk round it and under it.

The painting only ever shows one side of things. The bridge’s far side wears its near side, as a symmetric bridge would; where the painting is hidden behind something, the gap is filled with its own blank water and silk.

Your walker is a porter with two baskets on a shoulder pole, borrowed from the street outside Dr. Zhao’s shop near the end of the scroll. He always turns to face you, like a paper cut-out.
